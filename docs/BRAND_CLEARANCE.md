# Preliminary name and brand diligence

Date: 2026-07-25
Owner: Wilfred Grainger
Status: preliminary research only; not legal clearance

## Scope checked

The preliminary search used the word forms:

- `Cave Pony`
- `CavePony`
- `CAVE PONY`

The review considered the product name and the cave-and-pony logo concept for a coding-agent skill and possible related software, hosted software, documentation, and training uses.

## Official search routes

Further clearance must use the interactive official databases and similarity tools, not ordinary web indexing alone:

- [UK Intellectual Property Office trade mark search](https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark)
- [European Union Intellectual Property Office search](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/search-ip)
- [United States Patent and Trademark Office search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search)
- [USPTO design search codes](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/design-search-codes)
- [WIPO Nice Classification](https://www.wipo.int/classifications/nice/en/)

The UK service supports searches by keyword, phrase, or image. USPTO guidance requires searches for identical and confusingly similar wording, alternative spellings and pronunciations, related goods and services, and design elements. An exact-word search alone is not a comprehensive clearance search.

## Preliminary observations

No obvious indexed exact-name software product or coding-agent project was found in the initial web and official-domain search. This is not evidence that no conflicting registered, pending, unregistered, local, or recently filed right exists.

Existing non-software uses of the phrase were found, including:

- Cave Pony Club, an equestrian organisation in New Zealand;
- equine artwork and merchandise described as “Cave Pony” or “Cave Ponies”;
- unrelated creative and fan-art uses.

Those uses may be commercially or legally irrelevant, relevant to particular goods, or relevant to confusion and brand distinctiveness. A qualified reviewer must decide.

## Preliminary classes to examine

These are working search categories, not an application specification:

- Class 9: downloadable or recorded computer software and digital media;
- Class 42: software design and development, software as a service, and platform as a service;
- Class 41: education, training, and publication-related services if the brand is used for courses or structured learning.

Search related and coordinated classes as well as exact classes. Do not select application terms that the project does not genuinely plan to use.

## Logo review still required

The word search does not clear the visual mark. A design search should consider prominent elements such as:

- a pony or horse;
- a cave, rock arch, crystals, and torch;
- pixel-art presentation;
- the combined words and image.

Reverse-image search is not a substitute for official design-code and similarity review.

## Decision

The name may continue for the open-source pre-release with clear provenance and no claim of registration. Do not describe the name or logo as cleared, protected, registered, or commercially safe.

Before commercial promotion or a `v1.0.0` launch positioned as a durable brand:

1. complete interactive UK, EU, and US searches across words, variants, owners, classes, and designs;
2. record potentially similar results and why they are or are not relevant;
3. obtain advice from a suitably qualified trade mark professional where any similar mark or material uncertainty exists;
4. decide whether to proceed, narrow the intended goods and services, seek coexistence, or rename;
5. preserve the dated search record and decision owner.

This document records research and release risk. It is not legal advice.
